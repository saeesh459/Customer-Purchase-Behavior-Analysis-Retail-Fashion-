# Customer Purchase Behavior Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to identify patterns in purchasing habits, product preferences, revenue generation, discounts, payment methods, shipping preferences, and customer satisfaction.

The project transforms raw customer transaction data into actionable business insights using Microsoft Excel, data cleaning, Pivot Tables, and an interactive dashboard.

## Problem Statement
Retail businesses generate large volumes of customer transaction data. Raw data alone does not provide meaningful insights for decision-making.

This project analyzes customer purchase behavior to answer questions such as:
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which seasons perform best?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which payment and shipping methods are preferred?
- How do discounts influence purchases?
- Which purchase frequencies generate the most revenue?
- What insights can customer ratings provide?

## Key Insights
- The analysis identifies top-performing product categories and customer segments.
- Seasonal purchasing patterns help identify high- and low-performing periods.
- Payment and shipping preferences provide insights into customer convenience.
- Discount analysis helps evaluate promotional strategies.
- Purchase frequency and previous purchases provide insights into customer behavior.
- Customer review ratings provide an indication of overall customer satisfaction.

## Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing product categories.
2. Optimize inventory planning according to seasonal demand.
3. Use customer segmentation for personalized marketing campaigns.
4. Maintain convenient shipping and payment options.
5. Use targeted discounts instead of broad promotions where possible.
6. Develop retention strategies based on customer purchase cycles.
7. Perform deeper analysis of customer satisfaction at product and category levels.
